ot certainty, but confidence with a scope.

Not perfect memory, but records that expose their provenance.

Not uninterrupted strength, but practices that remain available below full capacity.

Not agreement, but a working arrangement capable of correction.

Not a promise that the next boot will succeed.

Enough architecture to try again.

When the May audit was finished, physical work was still waiting.

The project record identifies a fibre backhaul run of one hundred and eighty metres. Fifteen metres remained. The client and exact site do not belong here. Neither do invented weather, tools or scenery. The only detail this ending needs is the one abstraction cannot complete.

The last fifteen metres were physical.

No corrected timeline could pull them. No model could turn a finished paragraph into installed glass. The map could preserve the route and the machine could help reduce the cognitive load, but the substrate still required work in the world.

So the library was left in a state another reader could enter.

The correction was stored beside the error.

The next task did not need to become a metaphor before it could matter.

Fifteen metres remained.

I went back to the work.
