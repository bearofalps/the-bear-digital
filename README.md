# The Bear — Digital Review Copy

**What Survives the Restart**  
Marko Kolman  
Reader-review edition · 24 August 2026  
Digital packet · 25 August 2026

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is the book.

## What this is

A screen edition of the frozen manuscript. The sentences were not rewritten for the packet. The plates sit at the part breaks and at a few load-bearing chapters. They use the same grammar as the cover: rain, signals, empty stock, an occupied box, routes that still light after interruption.

## How to share

- Send the whole folder, or the zip.
- Offline reading works after fonts load once. If the network is gone, the text still sets in the system serif.
- Print from the browser if someone wants paper without the A5 press file.

## What this is not

- Not the shop interior. The A5 Typst folio remains the print twin.
- Not a claim that the images depict events in the book.
- Not a finished trade edition.
